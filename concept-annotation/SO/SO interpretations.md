**BEARER OF ATTRIBUTE**: Indicates that the annotation class should be interpreted as referring to the bearers of the given sequence attributes.
    -nucleic_acid (SO_0000348) and its subclasses
	-sequence_location (SO_0000735) and its subclasses

**MATERIAL OR INFORMATIONAL REPEAT REGION OR UNIT**: Indicates that the annotation class can be interpreted as referring to either material repeat regions or units or their corresponding informational repeat regions or unit.
	- repeat_region (SO_0000657) and its subclasses

**MATERIAL OR INFORMATIONAL SEQUENCE ENTITY**: Indicates that the annotation class can be interpreted as referring to either material sequence entities or their corresponding informational sequence entities.
	- sequence_collection (SO_0001260) and its subclasses
	- sequence_feature (SO_0000110) and its subclasses except for repeat_region (SO_0000657) and its subclasses
	- sequence_variant (SO_0001060) and its subclasses
